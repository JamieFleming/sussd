Welcome to Suss'd. This Privacy Policy explains how the Suss'd mobile application handles information when you use the game. By using Suss'd, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
🔍

Information We Collect

Suss'd is designed to require minimal user information.

Players may optionally enter:

  • Player names
  • Custom game words or phrases

This information:

  • Is stored locally on the device only
  • Is not uploaded to our servers
  • Is not shared with third parties
  • Is not permanently stored in the cloud
No account required. We do not require account creation, login details, email addresses, or phone numbers.
📱

Local Device Storage

Some game settings and preferences may be stored locally on your device to improve gameplay experience. This may include:

  • Saved settings
  • Unlocked bundles
  • Game preferences
  • Locally created custom word packs

This data remains on your device unless deleted by the user or removed when uninstalling the app.

Advertising

Suss'd may display advertisements using third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect limited anonymous data, including:

  • Device identifiers
  • Approximate location
  • Ad interaction data
  • Diagnostics and performance data

This information is used to display relevant ads, measure ad performance, and improve the advertising experience.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

🛒

In-App Purchases

Suss'd may offer optional paid content including premium word packs, special game modes, cosmetic features, and ad removal.

Purchases are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not receive or store:

  • Credit card information
  • Banking information
  • Payment credentials

All payment processing is handled entirely by the platform provider.

Analytics & Diagnostics

In the future, Suss'd may use analytics tools to help improve the app experience. These tools may collect anonymous technical information such as:

  • Crash reports

  • Gameplay session length
  • Feature usage

This information does not personally identify users.
